# Готель «Гетьман»

Live site: https://hetman.webart.work

## About
Готель «Гетьман» is a small, camerny (intimate) hotel with 14 rooms located on Польський ринок (Polish Market Square), 8, in Кам'янець-Подільський, Ukraine. The building has a 300-year history and keeps the atmosphere of the Old Town. The hotel positions itself as a compact, unhurried alternative to large hotel complexes, close to the historic square and the old city.

## Amenities
- 14 rooms (standard, family, and suite/luxe formats)
- Breakfast restaurant with five menu options
- Finnish sauna
- Location on the historic Польський ринок square
